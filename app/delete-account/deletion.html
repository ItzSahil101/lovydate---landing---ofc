Delete Your Lovydate Account

You can delete your Lovydate account from the account settings in the app. Account deletion removes your account and associated personal data from our active systems when the deletion is completed.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email support@lovydate.fun with the subject “Lovydate Account Deletion Request”.

For your safety, send the request from the email address associated with your Lovydate account where possible. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you own the account. Do not send your password.

What happens to your data?

When deletion is completed, we delete your account and associated personal data from active systems immediately. Limited information may remain temporarily in backups before they are overwritten, or may be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal claims, or enforcement of our rules. Any retained information is limited to those purposes and deleted when no longer needed.

Privacy questions: support@lovydate.fun.
